Energy & the inflation pass-through

Energy is the macro wildcard in CRE underwriting that has nothing to do with real estate. Crude feeds headline inflation — which, unlike core, includes food and energy — so when oil runs hot, headline PCE pulls above core, the Fed stays cautious, and long-end yields stay sticky. Since new CRE debt is priced off the 10-year, an oil shock lands on financing costs regardless of what property fundamentals are doing. Watch Brent alongside the headline-vs-core PCE gap below.
